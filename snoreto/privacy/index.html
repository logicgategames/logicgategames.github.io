Privacy Policy

Snoreto - LogicGateGames LLC

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LogicGateGames LLC handles information when you use Snoreto (the "App"), an Android app that records sound while you sleep, finds snoring and other sounds in the recording, and shows you when they happened. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your Recordings Never Leave Your Phone

Your recordings, clips, notes and settings stay on your phone, in the App's private storage. There is no account, no sign-in, no server and no upload. We operate no servers for your recordings, so there is nowhere for them to be sent.

The App uses the internet for two things only: to talk to Google Play about your Snoreto Pro purchase or subscription, and to send a crash report if the App crashes. Your recordings and clips are not part of either.

2. The Microphone

The App uses the microphone only while you choose to record. While it records, Android shows a notification, and on Android 12 and later the system shows its microphone indicator. The App asks for the microphone permission before your first recording, after explaining why. You can withdraw it at any time in Android's settings.

The sound is analysed on your phone by the App's own signal processing. There is no AI model and no cloud service involved, and nobody, including us, can listen to your recordings.

3. What The App Saves

  • Short clips of the sounds it detects, such as snoring.
  • If you turn it on, a full-night recording.
  • A record of how loud it was for each second of the night.
  • Tags you add (remedies and factors) and notes you write.

You can delete any night at any time. Clips are deleted automatically after the time you set in Settings. Everything the App stores is removed when you uninstall it.

4. Export, Import And Sharing

You can export all your nights to one file. You choose where it is saved in Android's own file picker, and you can import it again later. The file goes only where you put it, and from then on it is yours to look after.

With Snoreto Pro you can share a clip using the Android share sheet. Once you pick an app, that app's own privacy policy applies to the clip.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone - to record while you sleep.
  • Notifications - to show the recording notification and, if you turn it on, a bedtime reminder.
  • Foreground service - to keep recording while the screen is off.
  • Wake lock - to keep the phone awake enough to record all night.
  • Internet - only for crash reports and Google Play Billing.

6. Information Collected By The Services In The App

We do not collect your name, email address or phone number, and we do not sell personal information. The App shows no ads and does not use an advertising ID. The Google services built into the App do collect some data, and we list them here because having no server of our own is not an exemption:

  • Firebase Crashlytics - If the App crashes, a report is sent so we can fix the problem. It contains the technical details of the crash, the device model, Android version, app version, a random installation ID and which app installed Snoreto (for example Google Play). Reports contain no name, email or advertising ID, and none of your recordings. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Google Play Billing - Handles Snoreto Pro: the monthly and yearly subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase. Google handles the payment and tells the App whether you have Pro. We never see your card or payment details.

7. Backup

The App is excluded from Android's cloud backup. To move to a new phone, use Export and Import (section 4).

8. Children

Snoreto is a general-purpose app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Other People In The Room

A recording can pick up other people who are in the room. See the Terms of Use for what we ask of you.

10. Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
