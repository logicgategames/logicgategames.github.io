Snoreto: Snore Recorder

See when you snored, how long and how loud. Recordings never leave your phone.

Snoreto is a snore recorder for Android phones and tablets. Put your phone near the bed, tap Start recording and go to sleep. In the morning you open a report that shows when you snored, for how long and how loud.

There is no account. Your recordings, clips and notes stay on your phone. Snoreto records sounds for your own information. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything.

What it does

Morning report

Snoring time, the share of the recording, the number of snores and the peak loudness, for each night. You can add a note to any night.

Loudness timeline

A timeline of the whole night with every snore highlighted. A list of the sounds heard shows the time and length of each one. Other noises are marked apart from snoring.

Hear and share clips

Play back any snore clip straight from the report. Share a clip with someone you trust, or with your doctor, using the Android share sheet.

Tags and night comparison

Tag each night with what you tried, such as side sleeping, nasal strips, a raised pillow or a mouthguard, or with habits such as alcohol or a late meal. Put any two nights side by side.

Trends over weeks

See snoring per night over weeks. For each tag you get plain averages for the nights with it and without it, so you can compare them yourself.

Soft noise for sleeping

Play white, pink or brown noise made on your phone, with a volume control and a timer. Snoreto does not count snores while the noise plays, and the timeline shows that stretch.

Made for the night

Start in one tap, or set a start delay so you can fall asleep first. Choose a time for the recording to stop on its own, and set a bedtime reminder. A dark theme is easy on the eyes.

Export and import your nights

Export your nights, clips and settings to one file that you save where you choose, and import it on another phone. Old clips can be deleted automatically after a time you pick.

Where your data lives

Your recordings, clips, notes and settings stay on your phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account, no sign-in and no upload, and Snoreto runs no server for your recordings. The sound is analyzed on the phone by the app's own signal processing, with no AI model and no cloud service. The microphone is used only while you record. The app saves short clips of the sounds it finds, a record of the loudness for each second of the night, and your tags and notes. If you turn it on, it also keeps the whole night as audio. You can delete any night, and everything is removed when you uninstall the app. The app uses the internet only to talk to Google Play and to send an automatic crash report from Firebase Crashlytics if it crashes. A crash report holds technical details of the crash and the device, never your recordings. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for the app, so use Export and Import to move to a new phone. When you share a clip, the app you pick receives it and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Questions

Does Snoreto diagnose sleep apnea or any medical condition?

No. Snoreto records sounds for your own information. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose snoring, sleep apnea or any other condition. If you are worried about your snoring or your sleep, talk to a doctor.

Do my snore recordings get uploaded?

No. Recordings, clips and notes stay on your phone. There is no account and no server for them. The app uses the internet only for Google Play and for crash reports, and a crash report never contains your recordings.

How does Snoreto tell snoring from other noise?

It analyzes the sound on your phone with signal processing, not an AI model. It looks for a rhythmic run of snore-like sounds. Other sounds are marked apart from snoring. It can be wrong, so listen to the clips yourself.

Where should I put the phone, and will it last all night?

Put it near the bed, screen down, within reach of your pillow, and plug it in. Recording continues with the screen off. Battery saving settings on some phones can stop a long recording, and Snoreto shows what to change.

Can I use Snoreto while white noise plays?

Yes. Snoreto can play white, pink or brown noise. It does not count snores while the noise plays, and the timeline marks that stretch.

Can I see if something I tried reduced my snoring?

You can tag nights, compare any two nights and see averages for nights with and without a tag. The numbers do not say what caused a difference. They only show what the app heard.

What happens if I get a new phone or uninstall the app?

Uninstalling removes all your nights, clips and notes. There is no cloud copy. Before you switch phones, use Export in Settings to save one file, then use Import on the new phone.

What if another app uses the microphone during the night?

Snoreto cannot hear anything while another app or a call has the microphone. The report notes the time range when nothing was heard, and Snoreto listens again when the microphone is available again.
