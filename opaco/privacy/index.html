Privacy Policy

Opaco - Hide faces and private details - LogicGateGames

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LogicGateGames handles information when you use Opaco. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your Photos and Videos Never Leave Your Device

Opaco covers faces, number plates, sensitive text and QR codes in a photo or video before you share it. Every part of that happens on your phone:

  • Detecting faces, reading text, and finding barcodes, using detection models that are built into the App itself.
  • Deciding which text is sensitive - card numbers, IBANs, email addresses, phone numbers and number plates - which is done by checking the text against patterns and checksums on your device.
  • Drawing the masks and writing the new photo or video file.

No photo, video, frame, detected face, or piece of recognised text is ever uploaded or transmitted anywhere. We operate no servers and have no account system, so there is nowhere for your media to be sent even in principle.

The App does not request the Internet permission at all. This is not a policy promise - it is enforced by the App's manifest, and you can verify it on the Play listing's permission list.

2. Information We Collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, or account details. There is no sign-in of any kind, no analytics, and no crash-reporting service. The App contains no advertising.

We do not sell data, and we have no data to sell.

3. What Is Stored on Your Device

Opaco stores almost nothing. It keeps a small count of how many files you have saved, used only to decide whether to show the Play Store rating prompt, and a flag recording that the prompt has already been shown. It keeps no library index, no history of what you have edited, and no copies of your media. All of it is removed when you uninstall the App.

4. How Files Are Opened and Saved

Opaco requests no storage or gallery permission. You choose one file at a time through Android's own Photo Picker, or through the system file browser, and the App receives access to exactly the file you chose and nothing else. It cannot see the rest of your library.

Saving always writes a new file to your Pictures or Movies folder. Your original is never modified, moved or deleted.

5. Location and Metadata

Photos and videos often carry hidden metadata, including the GPS coordinates of where they were taken. Opaco does not read that data to learn anything about you, and the files it saves do not carry it forward: exported photos are written without EXIF or GPS tags, and exported videos are written into a fresh container that contains no location, device or original-timestamp information.

The App declares no location permission of any kind.

6. Permissions

Opaco requests no runtime permissions at all. It does not ask for photos and videos access, storage access, camera, location, or network. Everything it does is reached through the file you explicitly hand it.

7. What Opaco Cannot Promise

Automatic detection is not perfect. It can miss a face that is turned away, partly hidden, or in motion, and it can occasionally mark something that is not a face. The App is built around this: it shows you what it found, lets you add or remove masks yourself, and tells you plainly that faces can be missed. Always review the result before you share it.

Solid fill is the only mask style that cannot be reversed by software. Blur and pixelate remove the same detail, but as a matter of principle they are weaker: they are a visual effect, not redaction. Use solid fill when it matters.

8. Children

Opaco is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version of the App ever adds a feature that transmits data, this policy will say so explicitly and in advance of that version being released.
