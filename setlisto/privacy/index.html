Privacy Policy

Setlisto: Sheet Music Reader - LogicGateGames LLC

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LogicGateGames LLC handles information when you use Setlisto (the "App"), a sheet music reader for Android that shows your own PDFs and images, keeps them in setlists, and turns pages with a tap, a swipe or a Bluetooth pedal. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your Music Never Leaves Your Phone

Your sheet music, your setlists, your pen marks and your bookmarks stay on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud. We operate no servers for your music, so there is nowhere for it to be sent. The App has no advertising.

The App uses the internet for two things only: to talk to Google Play about the one-time purchase, and to send a crash report if the App crashes. Your music files, their names and your notes are not part of either.

2. Which Files Setlisto Can Read

Setlisto reads only the folders and files you choose in Android's own file picker. Android then lets the App read them again later, so your library opens without asking each time; it does not let the App see anything else on your phone. Setlisto asks for no storage permission. It never changes, moves, copies or deletes your files: your PDFs and images stay exactly where you keep them. You can stop the App reading a folder at any time in Settings, under "Music folders".

3. Your Pen Marks And Notes

Pen and highlighter marks, bookmarks, song details (title, composer, key, tags) and setlists are stored in the App's own storage, never written into your files. Your PDF itself is never changed.

4. Bluetooth Pedals And Keyboards

A Bluetooth page-turn pedal works like a keyboard: Android passes its key presses to the App while you read. Setlisto uses them only to turn pages and songs, and does not record them. The App does not use Bluetooth itself: you pair the pedal in Android's settings.

5. Information Collected By The Services In The App

We do not collect your name, email address or phone number, and we do not sell personal information. The Google services built into the App do collect some data, and we list them here because having no server of our own is not an exemption:

  • Firebase Crashlytics - If the App crashes, or hits certain errors it recovers from, a report is sent so we can fix the problem. It contains the technical details of the error, the device model, Android version, app version, a random installation ID and which app installed Setlisto (for example Google Play). Reports contain no name, email or advertising ID, and none of your music, file names or notes. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Google Play In-App Review - Once, after you have played through a whole setlist, the App may ask Google Play to show its rating sheet. Your rating goes to Google Play; the App is not told whether or how you rated it.
  • Google Play Billing - Handles the one-time Setlisto Pro purchase. Google tells the App whether you own it. We never see your card or payment details.

6. What Is Stored On Your Device

  • Your library: for each file, its name, size and date, and the song details and page ranges you set.
  • Your setlists, pen marks, bookmarks, the crop and zoom of each song, and the song and page you were on.
  • Your settings: language, appearance, night mode, page-turn mode, tap layout and pedal keys.
  • Whether you have bought Setlisto Pro.

The App is excluded from Android's cloud backup. Settings has an Export for your library and one for your settings, which save a file where you choose, for a new phone. Everything the App stores is removed when you uninstall it; your music files stay, because they are yours.

7. Children

Setlisto is a general-purpose music reader. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
