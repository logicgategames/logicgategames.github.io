2. Voice Follow

Voice follow moves the script by matching what it hears to the words of your script. It works best in a quiet room, reading in the language you set for the script. Background noise, music, other voices, a strong accent or words that are not in the script can make it wait, or move to a different place; you can always scroll the text by hand or switch to a fixed speed. Voice follow is available in English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Hindi and Russian; every language except English is downloaded from Google Play the first time it is needed.
