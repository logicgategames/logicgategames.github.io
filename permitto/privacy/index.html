Privacy Policy

Permitto: US Permit Test Prep - LogicGateGames LLC

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LogicGateGames LLC handles information when you use Permitto (the "App"), a study app for the US learner's-permit written test. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your Progress Stays On Your Device

Permitto keeps your study progress in a private database on your phone: the state you chose, the questions you answered and whether you got them right, and your exam scores. There is no account, no sign-in and no sync. We operate no servers for your progress, so there is nowhere for it to be sent.

The App does use the internet: to fetch advertisements, to ask for your ad consent where the law requires it, to talk to Google Play about purchases, and to send a crash report if the App crashes. Your answers and scores are not part of any of these.

2. Information Collected By The Services In The App

We do not collect your name, email address or phone number, and we do not sell personal information. The Google services built into the App do collect data, and we list them here because having no server of our own is not an exemption:

  • Google AdMob - Shows ads to users who have not bought Pro. Collects the Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier), ad interaction data, device model, operating system version, and approximate location derived from your IP address. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Google User Messaging Platform - Where the law requires it, asks for your consent to personalised ads and stores your answer on your device. You can change your answer at any time in the App's Settings, under "Ad privacy choices".
  • Google Firebase Analytics - Records basic, non-identifying usage of the App (for example that it was opened) under a random app-instance ID. It is configured so that advertising-related signals are denied until you have answered the consent request where one is required, and follow your answer afterwards. It never receives your answers, your scores or the state you study for.
  • Firebase Crashlytics - If the App crashes, a crash report is sent so we can fix the problem. It contains the technical details of the error, the device model, Android version, app version and a random installation ID. It contains no name, email or advertising ID and none of your study data. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Google Play Billing - Handles the one-time Pro purchase. Google tells the App whether you own it. We never see your card or payment details.

3. What Is Stored On Your Device

  • The state you are studying for, your language and appearance settings.
  • Each answer you give (which question, right or wrong, and when) and your exam results, used to show your readiness, your mistakes and your progress by topic.
  • Whether you have bought Pro, and your ad-consent answer.

Your progress may be included in Android's own device backup if you have it turned on, so that it can come back on a new phone; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account. Your purchase status and your consent answer are not included - a purchase is restored from Google Play, and consent is asked again. Everything is removed when you uninstall the App.

4. Export And Import

You can save your progress to a file and load it on another phone. This only happens when you ask for it, and Android asks you where the file goes. Once saved, the file is under your control; if you put it somewhere that syncs to a cloud service, that service's terms apply to it. The file never contains your purchase - Pro is restored from Google Play.

5. Read Aloud

If you use Read aloud, the App passes the question on screen to your phone's text-to-speech service, chosen in Android's settings. That service, not Permitto, handles the speech, under its own terms.

6. Reporting A Problem With A Question

If you report a question, the App opens your email app with a message addressed to us that contains the question's reference number. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and we use it only to check and correct the question.

7. Children

Permitto is meant for people preparing for a learner's permit, who are at least 14 to 16 years old in most states. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Not A Government App

Permitto is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any state DMV or government agency. Its questions are written by us from each state's official driver handbook.

9. Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
