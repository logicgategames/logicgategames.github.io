Privacy Policy

QuiNotes: Notepad & Memo - LogicGateGames

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LogicGateGames handles information when you use QuiNotes. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your Notes Stay On Your Device

QuiNotes keeps your notes in a private database on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no sync. We operate no servers for your content, so there is nowhere for your notes to be sent.

The App does use the internet, and we will not pretend otherwise: it is required to fetch advertisements and to talk to Google Play about purchases. Your notes are not part of either exchange.

2. Information We Collect

We collect nothing ourselves. We have no analytics, no crash reporting, and no account system. We never see your notes, their titles, your photographs, or your voice recordings.

The advertising service we use does collect data inside the App, and we list it here because "we have no server" is not an exemption:

  • Google AdMob - Displays ads. Collects the Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier), ad interaction data, device model, OS version, and approximate location derived from your IP address. You can reset this ID in your device settings.
  • Google Play Billing - Handles purchases. Google tells the App which items you own. We never see your card details, and no payment information reaches us.

Nothing from a note - not its text, its title, its photographs, its recordings, nor whether it is locked - is included in any ad request or purchase check.

3. What Is Stored On Your Device

  • Your notes, checklists, categories, tags, and the paper each note wears.
  • Photographs and voice recordings you attach, saved as the App's own private copies.
  • Any reminder you set, and when it should next arrive.
  • Your preferences, and whether you have purchased the ad removal or the full unlock.

This data is readable only by the App. It is not included in Android's cloud backup, and it is removed when you uninstall QuiNotes.

4. Photographs And Voice Recordings

When you attach a photograph, QuiNotes asks Android's photo picker for that single image. It does not request access to your photo library, so it cannot see any picture you have not chosen.

The App saves its own copy inside its private storage, so your note still shows the picture if you later move or delete the original. Voice recordings are made only while you are holding the recorder open, and are written to the same private storage. Neither is ever transmitted.

5. Locked Notes

You can lock any note, free of charge. The lock uses your device's own credential - fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern - checked by Android. QuiNotes never receives your fingerprint or face data; it asks Android whether the check passed and is told only yes or no. We store no PIN, password, or hash of our own.

A locked note is never previewed where someone else might see it: not in a notification, not on the home-screen widget, and not in search results.

Please note that a lock is not encryption. A backup file you create yourself contains your locked notes in readable form, and the App tells you so before it writes one.

6. Backups And Exports

QuiNotes can write your notes to a file, and this only ever happens when you ask for it. Android asks you to choose where the file goes. Once saved, that file is outside the App and under your control - if you place it somewhere that syncs to a cloud service, that service's own privacy terms apply to it. QuiNotes itself uploads nothing and has no access to any cloud account.

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state - Used to fetch advertisements and to reach Google Play for purchases. Never used to send your notes.
  • Notifications - Used to show the reminders you set. If you decline, the App works normally and simply does not notify you.
  • Alarms and reminders - Lets a reminder arrive at the time you asked for rather than whenever the system next chooses to wake the App. Optional; without it reminders still arrive, just less precisely.
  • Run at startup - Used solely to restore your existing reminders after the device restarts, because Android clears scheduled alarms on reboot.
  • Microphone - Used only while you are recording a voice memo into a note. Nothing is recorded in the background, and the recording stays on your device.
  • Vibration - Used for brief haptic feedback.

QuiNotes requests no other permissions. In particular it does not request storage access, location, contacts, or your photo library.

8. Advertising

Ads are shown via Google AdMob. You can opt out of personalised ads in device Settings under Google - Ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you will be asked for your advertising consent choices when you first open the App, and you can change them at any time from the App's own Settings screen.

Ads can be removed permanently with a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription.

9. Children

QuiNotes is not directed at children. The App is not marked as child-directed for advertising purposes, and we collect no information from anyone ourselves.

10. Your Rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give you a copy of, correct, or erase. Everything is on your device: you can export or back it up at any time from within the App, and uninstalling removes all of it. To reset the advertising identifier, use your device's own Google settings.

11. Changes To This Policy

We may revise this policy. The date above reflects the latest version, and any material change will be reflected in the App before it takes effect.
