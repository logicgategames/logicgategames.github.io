Privacy Policy

Diurna - Daily Planner, Journal - LogicGateGames

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LogicGateGames handles information when you use Diurna. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Diurna Cannot Send Your Writing Anywhere

This is not a promise about what we choose to do. Diurna does not request the Android internet permission at all, so the App has no ability to open a network connection - not for your entries, not for analytics, not for anything. Everything you write, and every photograph you attach, stays on your device.

We operate no servers and there is no account, no sign-in, and no sync. There is nowhere for your journal to be sent, even in principle.

2. Information We Collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, or any account details. There is no analytics service, no crash-reporting service, and no advertising in the App. We receive nothing about you or your use of Diurna.

3. What Is Stored On Your Device

Diurna keeps a private database on your phone containing the things you put in it:

  • The pages you open, and what you write on them.
  • Any reminder you set, and when it should next fire.
  • Your preferences, such as the page styles you have chosen.

This data is readable only by the App. It is not included in Android's cloud backup, and it is removed when you uninstall Diurna.

4. Photographs

When you attach a photograph, Diurna asks Android's photo picker for that single image. It does not request access to your photo library, so it cannot see any picture you have not chosen.

The App then saves its own copy of that image inside its private storage. It does this so your journal entry still shows the picture if you later move or delete the original from your gallery. That copy is stored on your device only, is never transmitted, and is deleted when you remove the photograph from the page or uninstall the App.

5. Exporting Your Writing

Diurna can write your journal to a file. This only ever happens when you ask for it, and Android asks you to choose where the file is saved. Once saved, that file is outside the App and under your control - if you place it somewhere that syncs to a cloud service, that service's own privacy terms apply to it. Diurna itself does not upload it.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications - Used to show the reminders you set. If you decline, the App works normally and simply does not notify you.
  • Alarms and reminders - Lets a reminder arrive at the time you asked for rather than whenever the system next chooses to wake the App. Optional; without it reminders still arrive, just less precisely.
  • Run at startup - Used solely to restore your existing reminders after the device restarts, because Android clears scheduled alarms on reboot.
  • Biometrics - Used only if you switch on the optional app lock, so Android can check your fingerprint, face, or screen lock before opening your journal. Diurna never receives your fingerprint or face data; it asks Android whether the check passed and is told only yes or no. We store no PIN, password, or hash of our own.

Diurna requests no other permissions. In particular it does not request internet access, storage access, location, contacts, or your photo library.

7. Children

Diurna is not directed at children, and because the App collects no information at all, no information from a child of any age is collected.

8. Your Rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give you a copy of, correct, or erase. Everything is on your device: you can export it at any time from within the App, and uninstalling removes all of it.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may revise this policy. The date above reflects the latest version, and any material change will be reflected in the App before it takes effect.
