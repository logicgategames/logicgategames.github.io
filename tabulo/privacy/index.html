Privacy Policy

Tabulo: Home Screen Widgets - LogicGateGames LLC

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LogicGateGames LLC handles information when you use Tabulo (the "App"), a collection of home-screen widgets for Android: clocks, dates, calendars, battery, countdowns, photos, contacts, search, app and settings shortcuts, notes and counters. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your Widgets Never Leave Your Phone

Everything you put in a widget - its design, colours and transparency, a note, a counter, a countdown, a photo, a contact - stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud. We operate no servers for your data, so there is nowhere for it to be sent.

The App uses the internet for two things only: to talk to Google Play about your "All designs" purchase, and to send a crash report if the App crashes. Nothing you put in a widget is part of either.

2. Contacts

A contact widget asks Android's own contact picker for one phone number. Android hands the App that one entry only; the App does not ask for permission to read your contacts and cannot see any others. The name and number (and the contact's photo, if Android lets the App read it) are copied into that widget's settings on your phone. Tapping the widget opens your phone app or messaging app with the number filled in; Tabulo never places a call or sends a message itself.

3. Photos

A photo widget uses Android's own photo picker. The App sees only the photo you pick, and keeps a copy of it in its own storage so the widget can show it. It asks for no storage or photo permission.

4. Apps, Search And Settings Shortcuts

To let you choose apps for a shortcut widget, the App lists the apps on your phone that have a launcher icon. That list is read on your phone when you open the picker and is never sent anywhere. Search widgets open Google search or voice search; what you type there goes to that app, not to Tabulo. Settings shortcuts open Android's own settings panels (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, volume and others); the App does not change any setting itself.

5. Information Collected By The Services In The App

We do not collect your name, email address or phone number, and we do not sell personal information. The Google services built into the App do collect some data, and we list them here because having no server of our own is not an exemption:

  • Firebase Crashlytics - If the App crashes, or hits certain errors it recovers from, a report is sent so we can fix the problem. It contains the technical details of the error, the device model, Android version, app version, a random installation ID and which app installed Tabulo (for example Google Play). Reports contain no name, email or advertising ID, and nothing you put in a widget. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Google Play Billing - Handles the one-time "All designs" purchase. Google tells the App whether you have it. We never see your card or payment details.

6. What Is Stored On Your Device

  • Each widget's design, colours, transparency and size, and what you put in it: note text, counter value and name, countdown event and date, world-clock cities, chosen apps, a contact's name and number, a photo.
  • Your settings: language and theme.
  • Whether you have bought "All designs".

The App is excluded from Android's cloud backup. Settings has an Export that saves your settings to a file where you choose, for a new phone. Everything the App stores is removed when you uninstall it.

7. Children

Tabulo is a general-purpose widget app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
