1. What PixPort Does

PixPort is an on-device image utility that crops, resizes, and compresses photos and signatures to match publicly published file specifications (such as 630 × 810 px / under 250 KB for photos, and under 100 KB for signatures) used by the Indian passport application portal, as well as a 2 × 2 inch format and several visa photo formats. It also runs advisory quality checks (face position, background, lighting, etc.), can take a photo with an optional guided in-app camera, and can lay out photos on a 4 × 6 print sheet.

Passing PixPort's checks does not guarantee that your photo or signature will be accepted by any government portal or authority. Official requirements may change without notice. Always verify the current requirements on the official portal before uploading. Acceptance decisions are made solely by the portal and the issuing authorities.

3. Quality Checks Are Advisory

PixPort's checks, including face position, background colour, sharpness and, for formats that require it, whether glasses are worn, are automated estimates made on your device. They can miss a problem or flag a photo that is acceptable. They are not connected to any government system, and a photo that passes them may still be rejected.
