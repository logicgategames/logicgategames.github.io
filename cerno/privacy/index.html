Privacy Policy

Cerno - Gallery Cleanup - LogicGateGames

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LogicGateGames collects, uses, and shares information when you use Cerno. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your Photos and Videos Never Leave Your Device

Cerno exists to help you clear space by reviewing the photos and videos already on your phone. All of that work happens on your device:

  • Reading your library, grouping it by month, and identifying screenshots or media received from messaging apps.
  • Finding near-identical photos, which is done by computing a small numeric fingerprint of each image on your device and comparing those numbers locally.
  • Measuring image sharpness to suggest which shot in a group is the one worth keeping.

No photo, video, thumbnail, filename, or fingerprint is ever uploaded or transmitted anywhere. We operate no servers and have no account system, so there is nowhere for your media to be sent even in principle.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, or account details. The App uses no sign-in of any kind, and we have no analytics or crash-reporting service. The only third party used by the App is:

  • Google AdMob - Displays ads. Collects the Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier), ad interaction data, device model, OS version, and approximate location derived from your IP address. You can reset this ID in your device settings.

Advertising data described above is shared with Google for advertising, ad measurement, and fraud prevention. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else. Nothing about your photos or videos is included in any ad request.

3. What Is Stored on Your Device

Cerno keeps a private database on your phone so it does not have to re-read your whole library each time. It records only identifiers and measurements - the system's own media IDs, file sizes, dates, folder names, and image fingerprints - never image or video content. This database is not included in device backups, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

4. Deleting Photos and Videos

Nothing is deleted unless you choose it and confirm. Items you select are moved to your device's system trash, where Android keeps them recoverable for 30 days before removing them permanently. Cerno never deletes anything on its own, on a schedule, or in the background.

5. Permissions

  • Photos and videos - Required to list what is on your device and to move items you select to the trash. Cerno needs to see your whole library because the sizes, groupings, and duplicate detection it reports are totals across all of it, which cannot be worked out from a few selected items. If you grant access to only some items, the App works with just those.
  • Internet - Used solely to request ads. Your media is never sent over it.
  • Media management (optional) - If you turn this on in system settings, Android stops asking you to confirm each cleanup. It is entirely optional, the App works fully without it, and you can turn it off at any time.

Cerno deliberately does not request access to media location data, so the GPS coordinates stored inside your photos are never read. It also does not request all-files access to your device storage.

6. Advertising

Ads are shown via Google AdMob. You can opt out of personalised ads in device Settings under Google - Ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you will be asked for your advertising consent choices when you first open the App, and you can change them later from the App's settings.

7. Children

Cerno is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
