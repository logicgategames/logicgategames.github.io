Last updated: August 2026 · LogicGateGames LLC

Sigla is a shortcut, not a safety device. It draws a button over other apps and can run actions like the torch, the camera or an alarm. Do not rely on it for anything time-critical or safety-critical: Android may stop a background service at any time, manufacturers restrict overlays differently, and a gesture can always be misread. Please read Sections 3 and 5.

1. What Sigla Does

Sigla is an on-device launcher. You teach it marks — shapes you draw — and attach them to actions: opening an app, running a system toggle, following a link, starting a timer. It also shows a floating button with a deck of shortcuts. Everything is stored and processed on your device. The app has no server and uploads nothing.

2. Free and Paid Features

Sigla is free to use, with an optional one-time in-app purchase that raises the limits on how many marks and deck buttons you can keep. The current free-tier limits and the price are always shown in the app before you buy. Purchases are processed by Google Play and are subject to Google Play's Terms of Service; refunds, where applicable, are handled according to Google Play's refund policy.

The purchase is one payment, not a subscription. It unlocks the app on the Google account that bought it.

3. Permissions You Grant

Some features need permissions that you grant yourself, and can withdraw at any time in Android Settings:

  • Display over other apps — for the floating button.
  • Accessibility service — for screenshots, navigation actions, the lock-screen button, and knowing which app is in front. It is configured so that it cannot read the content of your screen. See the Privacy Policy, section 4.
  • Modify system settings — only if you use a brightness or rotation-lock button.

Turning any of these off disables the features that depend on it. The rest of the app keeps working.
