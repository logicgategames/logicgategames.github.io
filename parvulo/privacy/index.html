Privacy Policy

Parvulo: Baby Book & Album - LogicGateGames LLC

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LogicGateGames LLC handles information when you use Parvulo (the "App"), a keepsake book where parents keep their baby's photos, videos, milestones and growth. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your Book Stays On Your Phone

Everything you put in your book is kept in the App's private storage on your phone: your children's names, birth or due dates and colors, the photos and videos you add (the App keeps its own copy; your originals stay exactly where they are), captions and notes, milestones, height and weight entries, and the cards and pictures you make. There is no Parvulo account and we operate no servers for your book, so we never receive it.

The App does use the internet: to fetch advertisements, to ask for your ad consent where the law requires it, to talk to Google Play about purchases, to send a crash report if the App crashes, to let Google Play services download the on-device cut-out tool used by Backdrops, and - only if you turn it on - to share your book through your own Google Drive (section 5).

2. Information Collected By The Services In The App

We do not collect your name, email address or phone number on any server of ours, and we do not sell personal information. If you turn on Google Drive sharing, the names, dates, photos, videos and notes in your book go to your own Google Drive (section 5); we never receive them. The Google services built into the App do collect data, and we list them here because having no server of our own is not an exemption:

  • Google AdMob - Shows ads to users who have not bought Pro. Collects the Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier), ad interaction data, device model, operating system version, and approximate location derived from your IP address. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID in your device settings. Ads never receive your photos, your children's details or anything else in your book.
  • Google User Messaging Platform - Where the law requires it, asks for your consent to personalized ads and stores your answer on your phone. You can change your answer at any time in the App's Settings.
  • Firebase Crashlytics - If the App crashes, or hits certain errors it recovers from, a report is sent so we can fix the problem. It contains the technical details of the error, the device model, Android version, app version and a random installation ID. Reports contain no name, email, advertising ID, photo or anything from your book. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Google Play In-App Review - After you have made a few keepsakes (an art card, a backdrop, a PDF book or an export), the App may ask Google Play once to show its rating sheet. Your rating goes to Google Play; the App is not told whether or how you rated it.
  • Google Play Billing - Handles the one-time Pro purchase. Google tells the App whether you own it. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Google Play services (Backdrops) - The cut-out that lifts your baby from a photo for a backdrop scene runs on your phone. Google Play services downloads the tool the first time it is needed; your photo is not sent anywhere.

3. Camera, Photos And Videos

Parvulo does not ask for access to your whole gallery. You choose photos and videos with Android's photo picker, and only the items you pick are copied into your book. "Take a photo" and "Record a video" open your camera app, and only the picture or video you keep comes back to Parvulo. Saving a picture to your gallery or sharing it happens only when you ask, through Android's own screens. When you share a photo, the copy that leaves the App has its location (GPS) information removed; the photo in your book is not changed.

4. Export And Restore

You can save your whole book as one ZIP file and restore it on another phone. This only happens when you ask for it, and Android asks you where the file goes. Once saved, the file is under your control; if you put it somewhere that syncs to a cloud service, that service's terms apply to it. Android's own device backup is turned off for Parvulo, so a ZIP file or Drive sharing is how a book moves to a new phone.

5. Sharing Through Your Google Drive (Optional)

If you turn on Drive sharing, the App asks Google for permission to use your Google Drive with the narrowest access Google offers (the "drive.file" permission): the App can see and change only the folder and files it creates, never the rest of your Drive. It then keeps a copy of your book in that folder: your children's names and dates, your memories with their captions and notes, and their photos and videos. Height and weight entries, your own custom milestones and your settings are not copied there; they travel only in the ZIP file from Export (section 4).

The folder belongs to your Google account and is stored by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy. If you share it with relatives, Google sends them the invitation and they see it in Google Drive; we never receive the folder, its contents or their addresses. You can stop sharing, turn Drive sharing off, or delete the folder in Google Drive at any time; you can also remove Parvulo's access in your Google Account settings.

6. What Is Stored On Your Phone

  • Your book (section 1), and the name you gave to sign your keepsakes, if any.
  • Your language, appearance and unit settings.
  • Whether you have bought Pro, and your ad-consent answer.

Everything is removed when you uninstall the App. Save a ZIP file or use Drive sharing first if you want to keep your book.

7. Deleting Your Data

You can delete any memory, or a child with everything about them, inside the App, and uninstalling the App deletes the whole book from your phone. Files in your Drive folder stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there. For crash reports or ad data, email us at the address below and we will help with what the services allow; we hold no other data about you.

8. Children

Parvulo is made for parents and family members, who are adults. It holds information about a child only because a parent chooses to record it, and that information stays on the parent's phone (and in their own Drive, if they turn sharing on). The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
